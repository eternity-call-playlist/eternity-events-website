How an event enquiry works

  1. Share the event brief: event type, date, city, venue, approximate guest count and desired experiences.
  2. Choose your services: shortlist event decor, production, entertainment or activities from the catalogue.
  3. Confirm feasibility: review availability, venue requirements, electricity, installation access and travel arrangements.
  4. Receive a quotation: confirm exact scope, inclusions, timing, payment terms and any additional charges in writing before booking.

For local enquiries, read our Hyderabad event services guide. For other cities, provide your exact location so the team can review availability and logistics; this page does not imply a physical office in every city.

Event management FAQs

Can I book one service instead of full event management?

Yes. Browse individual services and request a quotation for the option you need. Availability and minimum booking requirements are confirmed case by case.

Do you take enquiries outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Enquiries across India are welcome. Staff, equipment, transport and installation feasibility must be reviewed for each city and venue.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the date, city, venue, event type, estimated guests, preferred services, event hours and any special requirements.

Are the displayed service prices final?

Catalogue prices are indicative. The final written quotation depends on the services, event duration, transport, staffing and setup needs.
