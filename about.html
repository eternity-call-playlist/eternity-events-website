How booking works

  1. Share your brief: event type, date, city, venue, estimated guests and preferred services.
  2. Review options: shortlist relevant services and discuss technical or venue requirements.
  3. Confirm a quote: agree on available services, booking duration, equipment, staffing, setup and transport charges in writing.
  4. Prepare for the event: confirm access, power and any venue-side arrangements before the event date.

No two venues have exactly the same requirements. Displayed catalogue prices are indicative, and your confirmed quotation defines the service scope.
