Are photo strip prints unlimited?

What is the difference between a photo strip and a photo magnet?

A photo strip is a printed layout containing a sequence of pictures. A photo magnet is a magnetic photo keepsake designed for display on a suitable magnetic surface.

Can the strip include our event name or company logo?
